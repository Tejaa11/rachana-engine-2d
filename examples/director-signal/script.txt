Mira and her friend arrange a listening session in their little room beside their street. They imagine neighbors sharing stories, but their first poster is crowded with plans and promises. At the table, Mira reads it twice and still cannot find the simple invitation. Her friend slides a blank sheet between them and asks what matters most. Together, they keep one clear message: bring a story, or just come and listen. They leave space around those words and choose one quiet hour for everyone to meet. That evening, only two neighbors arrive, and the empty chairs feel bigger than expected. Then one visitor remembers a song from home, and the other begins to smile. Mira folds away the extra chairs, glad this small gathering can still leave room for people to connect.
