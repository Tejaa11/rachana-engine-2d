At her workbench, Mira studies a paper lantern that looks nothing like her sketch. She imagined a small glow above the terrace, but this first shape looks flat and tired. For one moment, she considers hiding the creased paper and starting again. Instead, Mira turns the lantern and notices one fold pulling its shape inward. She changes that fold, then pauses to look before changing anything else. The next attempt is uneven, yet now the little lantern has room to open. By evening, the revised shape rests beside her original sketch, and the workshop is quiet. Outside, a gentle glow fills its paper walls as the terrace settles into blue shadows. Mira keeps the sketch nearby, remembering how a patient revision gave her idea a life worth sharing.
